# Community-Biogas-Managment
A full-stack web application for assessing biogas plant feasibility, managing rural organic waste resources, monitoring biogas production, and promoting sustainable community-based renewable energy solutions.
# 🌱 Rural Community Biogas Feasibility and Resource Management System

## 📌 About the Project

The **Rural Community Biogas Feasibility and Resource Management System** is a web-based application designed to help rural communities evaluate the feasibility of establishing biogas plants and efficiently manage available organic waste resources.

The system provides a centralized platform to assess cattle dung availability, agricultural waste, and household biodegradable waste to estimate biogas production potential, recommended plant capacity, installation costs, and potential economic savings.

It also supports community-based resource sharing, biogas plant monitoring, seasonal resource analysis, and geographical visualization to encourage sustainable waste management and renewable energy utilization.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Evaluate the feasibility of biogas plant installation.
- Estimate biogas production based on available organic waste.
- Manage cattle dung and agricultural waste resources.
- Support community-based feedstock collection and sharing.
- Monitor biogas plant performance and operational conditions.
- Estimate installation costs, subsidies, and potential savings.
- Promote sustainable waste management and renewable energy.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Interactive Dashboard:** Displays households, available resources, biogas plants, and system analytics.
- **Biogas Feasibility Calculator:** Estimates plant capacity, production potential, installation costs, and savings.
- **Household Management:** Maintains household and cattle information.
- **Feedstock Management:** Tracks dung, agricultural waste, and organic waste availability.
- **Community Resource Sharing:** Supports resource pooling among participating households.
- **Plant Monitoring:** Tracks feedstock input, gas production, temperature, pH, and operational status.
- **Seasonal Resource Analysis:** Evaluates resource availability across different seasons.
- **Geographical Mapping:** Visualizes households, collection points, and biogas plant locations.
- **Cost-Benefit Analysis:** Estimates financial benefits and payback periods.
- **Environmental Impact Analysis:** Estimates potential environmental benefits.
- **Government Scheme Information:** Provides information about relevant biogas schemes and subsidies.
- **Reports and Analytics:** Generates downloadable reports and visual summaries.
- **User Authentication:** Supports role-based access for administrators, village coordinators, and household users.
